# World news, Saturday 21 November 2026 — in one image

A newspaper front page (1024×1024 PNG) summing up the world's news of
Saturday 21 November 2026, built for the make.fun bounty
[news-image-20261121](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261121).

Lead story: Saudi air defences intercept three Houthi ballistic missiles
fired at Riyadh in the early hours of Saturday — the second attack on the
capital in three days. Also on the page: NATO chief Mark Rutte visits
Warsaw after Russian missiles land in Poland, COP31 climate talks run
into overtime as fossil fuels vanish from the draft deal, a 6.9 quake
shakes Peru's Andes, Russian strikes kill 2 and injure 58 in Kryvyi Rih,
and Florida river flooding breaks a 1953 record.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the front page itself (1024×1024, under 1 MiB)
- `render.py` — the Pillow script that draws it (all artwork is original
  vector art; no photographs)
- `main.py` — entrypoint: copies `entry.png` to the output directory

## Rebuild

```sh
python3 render.py
```

Sources for the day's news: Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, BBC, CNN.
